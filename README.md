# F Studio - Professional Football Tactical Board & Formation System
**by Felix T Shyju, Franklin Abraham Mathew, Fahbin Jareer**
- A responsive, broadcast-grade football tactical board and squad management web application designed for coaches, analysts, and fans.

## ⚽ Key Features
- **Centered Top Header**: Sleek "F STUDIO" brand identity centered at the top with live squad badges and helper tools.
- **Dual Theme Support (White & Dark Theme)**: Seamless instant switching between UEFA broadcast dark mode and high-contrast daylight white theme.
- **Accurate Real-World Formations**:
  - `4-3-3 Attacking` (Single anchor pivot with advanced eights and high wingers)
  - `4-4-2 Classic` (Double banks of 4 with paired strikers)
  - `3-5-2 Midfield Control` (Dynamic wing-backs with back-3 foundation)
  - `4-2-3-1 Pivot` (Double pivot defensive stability with playmaker trio)
- **Team Captaincy Engine**:
  - Golden Captain armband (`C`) indicator on player token and kit.
  - Interactive Captaincy assignment in the player edit modal.
  - Dedicated Team & Captain spotlight card featuring Lionel Messi (#10, 94 OVR).
- **Interactive Drag & Drop & Substitutions**:
  - Free drag-and-drop repositioning with pointer capture and mobile touch support.
  - Click any player to edit name, number, position, and captaincy.
  - One-click tactical substitutions (`⇄ Sub`) from the 12-man bench roster with role filtering (`ALL`, `DEF`, `MID`, `FWD`, `GK`).
- **Helping Tools**:
  - Quick formation reset (`↺ Reset`).
  - Copy / Export starting XI lineup to clipboard (`📋 Export`).
  - Compact / Full player tags toggle (`🏷️`).
  - On-demand Tactical Guide modal (`❓ Guide`).
- **Official Copyright Footer**:
  - Professional footer with copyright notice and version metadata.
- **Fully Responsive**:
  - Precision layout on PC monitors, tablets, and smartphones.
  - Touch-optimized drag controls and tap targets.
